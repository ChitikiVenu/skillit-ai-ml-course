Module 5 — Agentic AI Engineering (4 Weeks)

Beyond single-shot LLM calls lies agentic AI — systems that reason, call tools, maintain state and execute multi-step workflows autonomously. This module builds and evaluates real AI agents designed to operate safely in production.

Who This Module Is For
Students ready to move from RAG applications into building autonomous, tool-using AI agents.
Real-World Relevance
Agentic AI is the newest and fastest-moving frontier in AI engineering — AI Agent Developer and Agentic AI Engineer roles are emerging as one of the most sought-after specializations in the field.
